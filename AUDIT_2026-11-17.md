## Local uniform-tremble branch

**Proposition (local, uniform floors only).** For all sufficiently small positive epsilon, the perturbed game with every action probability at least epsilon has an analytic equilibrium branch approaching the corner
`(b11,b21,b23,c11,c21,b41,a33,c33) = (0,1/4,0,0,1/2,1/2,1/2,3/4)`.
The branch is locally unique within the specified active pattern in the scaled coordinates below. This does **not** assert global uniqueness or selection for arbitrary positive tremble shapes.

**Active pattern.** Interior coordinates, in order:
`(a32,a33,a41,b21,b33,b41,c21,c33)`.
The ten upper-floor coordinates are
`a42,a43,a44,b42,b43,b44,c41,c42,c43,c44 = 1-epsilon`.
All other non-interior coordinates equal epsilon.

Write the interior coordinates as
`(epsilon*z0,z1,epsilon*z2,z3,z4,z5,z6,z7)`.
For each interior coordinate impose its own-reach-stripped gradient `D_i=0`.
Divide the c33 equation by epsilon (an exact polynomial factor).
Replace the b41 equation by
`(D_b41 + D_b21/2 + D_b33/2)/epsilon=0`.
This row operation is equivalent when epsilon>0 and the other mixed equations hold; its numerator is exactly divisible by epsilon.

At epsilon=0, the eight equations are:

```
(z3 + z5*z6 + 3*z6 - 2)/24 = 0
(2*z3 - z5)/12 = 0
-(z3 + z6 - z7)/12 = 0
-(2*z1 - 1)/12 = 0
(2*z6 - 1)/12 = 0
-(z0*z6 + 2*z1 - z2 - 7*z6 - 17)/24 = 0
-(2*z4 - z5 - 1)/12 = 0
-(z2 - 4)/12 = 0
```

They vanish at
`z*=(47,1/2,4,1/4,3/4,1/2,1/2,3/4)`.
Their Jacobian determinant there is exactly **1/214990848**, so the analytic implicit-function theorem supplies a locally unique branch of mixed-equation solutions.

Implicit differentiation gives
`z'(0)=(93,11/2,4,3/8,-325/8,13/4,-2,-39/8)`.
Thus:

| Coordinate | Expansion |
|---|---|
| a32 | 47 epsilon + 93 epsilon^2 + O(epsilon^3) |
| a41 | 4 epsilon + 4 epsilon^2 + O(epsilon^3) |
| a33 | 1/2 + (11/2) epsilon + O(epsilon^2) |
| b21 | 1/4 + (3/8) epsilon + O(epsilon^2) |
| b33 | 3/4 - (325/8) epsilon + O(epsilon^2) |
| b41 | 1/2 + (13/4) epsilon + O(epsilon^2) |
| c21 | 1/2 - 2 epsilon + O(epsilon^2) |
| c33 | 3/4 - (39/8) epsilon + O(epsilon^2) |

**Feasibility and incentives.** The six interior coordinates with nonzero limits remain strictly between epsilon and 1-epsilon. The other two have leading coefficients 47 and 4, both greater than 1, so they too remain strictly interior. All 40 pinned coordinates have the required strict gradient sign for sufficiently small positive epsilon. Thirty-nine are decided by a nonzero constant or linear term. The remaining coordinate has `D_c44=(5/4)epsilon^2+O(epsilon^3)>0`. Exact coefficients are in `audit-results/tremble-uniform-symbolic.json`.

Every information set is reachable under positive floors. Hence the mixed equalities and floor signs establish optimality at every decision in this perfect-recall perturbed game, giving a Nash equilibrium. By continuity its limit has beta=1/4, c33=3/4 and payoffs (-1/32,-1/48,5/96).

The algebra and signs are reproducible with `python makeD.py`, `python checkD.py`, then `python tremble_uniform_symbolic.py` (create `audit-results/` first, or use the audit driver).
