A. Answers to the exercises

1.2
\(u=(0,0,0)\). Each player holds the highest dealt card in 8 of the 24 deals (the 4 in 6, or the 3 when the 4 is undealt in 2), so each gets \(\kappa(8\cdot2-16\cdot1)=0\).
1.3
312. Per deal: after a bet by P1, 2×2 = 4 leaves; after K then P2's bet, 2×2 = 4; after KK, 1 (all check) + 2×2 = 4 (P3 bets). Total 4 + 4 + 5 = 13.
2.1–2.3
Both numerator and denominator flip sign. \(du_B+du_C=-du_A\) by Prop. 1. With two players \(du_B=-du_A\), so \(\rho=1\), and \(du_A=0\Rightarrow du_B=0\).
3
\(D_{a_{33}}=\frac{2b_{11}+2b_{21}-b_{41}}{12}\); \(D_{c_{21}}=-\frac{b_{11}+2b_{33}-b_{41}-1}{12}\); \(D_{a_{11}}=-\frac{2b_{32}+2c_{33}-1}{12}\); \(D_{b_{31}}=-\tfrac5{48}\) once \(c_{11}+c_{21}=\tfrac12\). \(D_{c_{44}}=5R_{c_{44}}\) and \(D_{c_{13}}=-R_{c_{13}}\) (Ch. 7.2).
4
Only \(a_{41}\) fails: \(D_{a_{41}}={{ex_c33_34}}>0\) while \(a_{41}=0\). Switching \(a_{41}\) from 0 to 1 raises \(u_1\) by exactly \(\tfrac1{384}\) (P1's own reach of the root is 1): with \(c_{33}=\tfrac34\) P3 calls P1's value bet with the 3 too often.
5.1
\(b_{33}=\tfrac12+\tfrac{b_{41}-b_{21}}2-b_{23}(1-b_{21})\).
5.3
Table 3 minus prose is \(\tfrac{b_{11}-b_{21}}2-b_{23}(1-b_{21})\); this is zero only if \(b_{11}=b_{21}\) and \(b_{23}=0\), or \(b_{23}\) is at its maximum.
6.1
\(du=(-\tfrac7{480},\tfrac{11}{480},-\tfrac1{120})\), so \(\rho_{1\to3}=-\tfrac47\) at \(c_{11}=\tfrac14\). At \(c_{11}=\tfrac1{10}\): \(-\tfrac{31}{70}\).
6.2
At \(c_{33}\)'s set P3 holds the 3, so no deal has P1 betting the 3 and reaching it.
7.1
At BF the pot before P3 acts is 4; calling puts in 1 more. Win: \(+3\); lose: \(-2\); fold: \(-1\). So call iff \(3q-2(1-q)>-1\iff q>\tfrac15\). Given P3 holds 3 and P2 folded, P2 folds the 1 and 2 but never the 4. The weights are therefore \(r_1\) (P2 holds 2), \(r_2\) (P2 holds 1) and \(2r_4\) (P2 holds 1 or 2), giving \(q=\frac{r_1+r_2}{r_1+r_2+2r_4}\).
7.3
\(\mathrm{lo}=\tfrac12\), \(\mathrm{hi}=\tfrac{23}{32}\), \(w=\tfrac7{32}\), \(\mathrm{lo}+w/3=\tfrac{55}{96}\).
